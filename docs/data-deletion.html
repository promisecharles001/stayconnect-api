STAYCONNECT NG - ACCOUNT & DATA DELETION

How to request deletion of your StayConnect NG account and the personal data
associated with it.

1. HOW TO REQUEST DELETION

Send an email to support@stayconnect.ng from the email address registered on
your account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Include:
- The full name on the account
- The registered email address
- The registered phone number

We verify that the request comes from the account owner before acting on it.
Requests from an address other than the registered one will be refused.

2. WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

We acknowledge your request within 7 days and complete it within 30 days.

3. WHAT IS DELETED

The following are permanently removed:
- Your name, email address, phone number and profile picture
- Your identity verification records, including uploaded ID documents and
  verification selfies
- Your saved payout bank details
- Your property listings, saved wishlist and push notification tokens
- Your messages and call records

4. WHAT IS RETAINED, AND WHY

If your account has booking, payment or payout history, those financial
records are retained rather than deleted. They are records of money that
moved between users, and Nigerian tax and anti-fraud obligations require us
to keep them. In that case we delete or anonymise the personal data listed in
section 3 and retain only the transaction records themselves.

Retained financial records are kept for 6 years from the date of the
transaction, after which they are deleted.

5. ACCOUNTS WITH ACTIVE BOOKINGS

An account with a booking that has not yet completed, or with funds held in
escrow or a withdrawal in progress, cannot be deleted until those are
settled. We will tell you what is outstanding and process the deletion once
it is resolved.

6. ALTERNATIVE TO DELETION

If you want to stop using StayConnect NG but keep your booking history, ask
us to suspend the account instead. A suspended account cannot be signed into
and is not visible to other users.
